Strong Closure, End-of-Quarter Carry-Over

98.4% of Q3 tickets closed with a solve time. Of the 15 still open, 13 were created in September and 9 in the final week (28–30 Sep) — normal carry-over, not an aged backlog. Only two predate September (Nextsmile since 11 Aug, Northshore Dental since 27 Aug).

Pending Tickets Awaiting Classification

The 7 tickets with no Category or Feature are all pending tickets that haven't been solved yet — they make up 7 of the 15 open tickets. Separately, 67 solved tickets (6.9%) were closed without an Organisation; Organisation will be assigned to all tickets going forward.

Resolution Time: Advaith on Business Critical

Advaith carries 41.2% of tickets but 52.7% of resolution hours. The gap is concentrated in Business Critical work: median 63.0 hrs on 32 tickets vs. 3.5 hrs (Elsaba) and 1.7 hrs (Nikhil) on similar volumes.

Opportunity — Holiday Calendar Self-Serve

Holiday Calendar alone is 246 tickets (25.5% of the quarter), median 0.9 hrs. Configuration / Admin is 55.6% of volume but just 31.6% of resolution time — fast, repeatable work that is a strong self-serve candidate.

Business Critical Long Tail

86 Business Critical / Phone Impacting tickets: median 5.2 hrs but P90 232.6 hrs. July was the outlier (51 BC tickets vs. 18 in Aug and 17 in Sep), driven by a Devices spike — 63 device tickets in July vs. 30 and 23 after.

Volume Settled After July

July peaked at 352 tickets, then eased to 302 (Aug) and 311 (Sep). Average resolution improved month on month: 48.8 → 44.2 → 37.0 hrs, and P90 fell from 127.1 to 94.7 hrs.

The 15 open tickets are spread across 13 practices. Only Dental Couture and Voicestack AU have 2 open; everyone else has 1. Small accounts read as high backlog % on a single open ticket, so read the counts alongside the rate.

Recommended Operating Actions — Q4 2026
  • 1
    Close Out Classification Gaps

    67 tickets were solved without an Organisation this quarter; Organisation will now be assigned to all tickets, so this should stop from Q4. The 7 tickets with no Category / Feature and 10 with no Ticket type are all pending tickets — make sure each one is fully classified when it's solved.

  • 2
    Business Critical Coaching — Advaith

    Median 63.0 hrs on 32 Business Critical tickets vs. 3.5 hrs (Elsaba) and 1.7 hrs (Nikhil). How-to tickets show the same pattern (16.2h vs. 3.3h / 2.3h). Review the BC tickets together to see where handoffs or waiting-on-customer time is building up, and consider rebalancing setup work (61 of 74 New Config requests sit with Advaith).

  • 3
    Pilot Holiday Calendar Self-Serve

    Holiday Calendar is 246 tickets (25.5% of all Q3 volume) at a 0.9-hr median. Together with Agent setup (106) and Agent App (116), these three features are 468 tickets (48.5%). A guided self-serve flow or customer enablement could deflect a large share.

  • 4
    Business Critical Fast-Track & Devices Root Cause

    86 Business Critical tickets have a P90 of 232.6 hrs. Devices is the biggest incident driver (116 tickets, P90 199.5 hrs) and spiked in July (63 vs. 30 / 23). Set up a fast-track queue with an SLA countdown and confirm what caused the July device spike so it can be prevented.

  • 5
    Chase the Two Aged Open Tickets

    #4348 Nextsmile (Integrations, pending since 11 Aug, Nikhil) and #4579 Northshore Dental (Bot Assisted Calls, open since 27 Aug, Advaith) are the only open tickets older than September. Two further DNI Campaign integration tickets (First Choice Dental, Voicestack AU) are open with Advaith.

  • 6
    Investigate Integration & Third-Party Delays

    The 13 Integration tickets have a median of 228.9 hrs (P90 452.2 hrs), and the 3 Twilio Ops tickets a P90 over 1,000 hrs. Check whether these are stuck with a third party or on an internal handoff, and agree an owner and escalation path.
